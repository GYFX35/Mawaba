# Mawaba Sponsorship & GitHub Partnership Program

Welcome to the **Mawaba Sponsorship and GitHub Partnership Program**! Mawaba is an open-source global interaction, education, climate, and development ecosystem built to empower communities, students, and businesses worldwide.

By sponsoring Mawaba through **GitHub Sponsors** or becoming an official GitHub Partner, you directly support open-source AI tutoring, global education access, climate action tools, and open tech innovation.

---

## 💖 Why Sponsor Mawaba?

Mawaba integrates AI tools with social impact initiatives, including:
- 🎓 **AI Master Tutor**: Free open access for students globally.
- 🌱 **Climate Solutions & CO2 Calculator**: Interactive tools promoting sustainability.
- 🌍 **Global Culture & Heritage Archive**: Preserving digital traditions and ancestral knowledge.
- 🛠️ **Developer Portal & Open APIs**: Empowering builders across emerging markets.

Your support helps us maintain cloud infrastructure, expand AI service integrations, award developer grants, and provide free tools to under-represented regions.

---

## 🏆 Sponsorship Tiers & Benefits

### 🥉 Individual Supporter ($5 / month)
- **Sponsor Badge**: Official Sponsor badge on your GitHub profile.
- **Community Wall**: Name listed in our `CONTRIBUTORS.md` and Sponsorship Hall of Fame.
- **Discord / Community Access**: Exclusive Supporter role in community channels.

### 🥈 Developer Champion ($25 / month)
- All Individual Supporter benefits.
- **Priority Issue Handling**: Priority review for submitted issues and feature requests.
- **Early Access**: Beta testing access for new AI models and microservice releases.

### 🥇 Corporate Partner ($250 / month)
- All Developer Champion benefits.
- **Logo Placement**: Prominent logo display on our README.md, website homepage, and docs.
- **Technical Workshop**: Bi-annual tech sync or Q&A session with the core engineering team.
- **Custom Integration Consultation**: Guidance on integrating Mawaba's open API suite.

### 💎 Strategic Global Partner ($1,000+ / month)
- All Corporate Partner benefits.
- **Premier Logo Feature**: Top-tier positioning as a Main Sponsor across all Mawaba platforms.
- **Joint Marketing & Case Study**: Dedicated blog post, PR release, and GitHub Partner story.
- **Board Advisory Guest**: Invitation to quarterly roadmap and open-source steering meetings.

## 🚀 How to Become a Sponsor via GitHub Sponsors

1. Navigate to our [GitHub Sponsors Page](https://github.com/sponsors/mawaba).
2. Select your preferred tier or custom amount.
3. Choose monthly or one-time payment.
4. Complete checkout through GitHub's secure platform.
